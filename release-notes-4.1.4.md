# Glide 4.1.4 — navigation crash diagnostic/stability release

## Highlights 🚀

- ⚡ **Launch Speed Boost** keeps Glide warm for faster image opening and preserves the established low-footprint standby experience.
- 🪟 **Windows Snap preservation** keeps edge snapping and Snap Layout behaviour across warm restores.
- 🛡️ **Privacy-safe warm restore** prevents the previous image or Home surface from flashing during transitions.
- 🔗 **Single warm instance** continues to prevent duplicate Glide processes and extra taskbar icons.
- 🖼️ **Same-image warm reopen** remains supported after closing and reopening the same picture.
- 🧰 **Installer safety check** detects a running Glide instance and offers to close it before installation.
- 🧭 **Transactional Home/End navigation** keeps the visible image, counter, and navigator index consistent when a target presentation is cancelled.
- 🧠 **Atomic viewport presentation** protects rapid navigation and refinement from stale or retired bitmap state reaching the compositor.
- 🛠️ **Expanded diagnostics** record frame swaps, render skips, held-playback cancellation, navigation generations, and edge-navigation rollback breadcrumbs.
